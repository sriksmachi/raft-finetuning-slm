# Conclusion

This guide offers a comprehensive overview of best practices for securing a ServiceNow instance and should be followed by customer administrators, security personnel, and compliance officers.

Organized around the six principles of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover), this guide offers actionable recommendations for customers to safeguard their ServiceNow instance, as well as an overview of the ServiceNow responsibilities regarding security.

By following these best practices and engaging relevant security stakeholders, organizations can strengthen their security posture, demonstrate due diligence, and maintain compliance across the ServiceNow AI Platform.
