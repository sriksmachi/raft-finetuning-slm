### Best practices continued

#### Know the difference between software updates and upgrades

Understanding the difference between a software update and an upgrade is essential for maintaining both security and support in your ServiceNow environment.

- **Software updates:** Patches and platform fixes delivered throughout the year via the ServiceNow Patching Program. These address known vulnerabilities and improve performance without changing the platform version.
- **Upgrades:** Moving to a newer major platform version, typically released twice a year, which includes enhanced features, architectural improvements, and broader functionality.

Both are critical but serve different purposes in keeping your instance secure, stable, and supported.

#### Adopt a proactive patch management process

Establish and follow a routine schedule to promptly install security patches and platform updates in both production and sub-production environments. Treat production and sub-production systems with equal diligence, ensuring that all environments are updated.

#### Ensure instances remain on a supported family release

ServiceNow releases two major version updates per year and only supports the current version (N) and one prior release (N-1).

Instances operating on older family release are categorized as “End-of-Life” (EOL) and do not receive new updates or patches. Organizations should establish and maintain a formal process to proactively track their ServiceNow instance’s family release version, ensuring timely upgrades before the platform reaches EOL status.
