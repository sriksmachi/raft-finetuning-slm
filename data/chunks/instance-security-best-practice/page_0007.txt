# GOVERN

## Security contacts

**Customer responsibility**  
As outlined in the DSA, customers are responsible for identifying and maintaining appropriate security contacts by identifying them in [ServiceNow Support Security Contacts](https://www.servicenow.com).

**Best practices**  

### Create a process to update security contacts regularly  
Ensure the security contact record within ServiceNow Support is always kept up to date, with details of at least two appropriate information security personnel. Set a reminder to review the security contact details at least quarterly to ensure they are current and accurate.  

- Security contacts should be authorized to act on the information they receive, because it may be critically important to the organization.  
- Use a distribution list in the security contact field when possible.  

**Additional information**  
- Update your [ServiceNow Support Security Contacts](https://www.servicenow.com) (requires a ServiceNow Support account)
