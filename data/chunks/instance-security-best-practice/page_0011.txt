# Physical security and environmental controls

### ServiceNow responsibility
ServiceNow performs colocation facility vendor reviews annually and periodically performs onsite assessments of physical and environmental controls.

ServiceNow colocation facility operations, security, and procurement personnel review the colocation facility provider SOC 2 Type 2 reports or other available assessments (e.g., ISO 27001) on an annual basis.

Additionally, through periodic visits to the facility, ServiceNow colocation facility operations and compliance personnel review the controls applicable to the services provided to ServiceNow.

- Routine meetings are held with vendor account management personnel to help ensure services are provided according to expectations.
- Cloud operations personnel review access activity within the colocation facility suites on a periodic basis to ensure access is appropriate.

### Best practices
**Review and understand physical security and environmental controls at ServiceNow**

Establish a process involving relevant stakeholders to review the physical security and environmental controls information.
